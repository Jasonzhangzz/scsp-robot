\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\rev{This work presented SCSP, a cascaded framework that separates contact-location selection from contact-trajectory optimization for contact-rich manipulation. CSO evaluates a finite set of sampled surface points with a surrogate contact model, and CPO uses the resulting contact prior together with a ranking rule to generate approaching, contact, and contact-switching trajectories. Simulations and hardware experiments show that this decomposition can improve manipulation diversity and task success under the tested geometric and modeling conditions. The results also indicate that SCSP remains limited by the point-contact and fixed-contact-set assumptions of the surrogate model, as well as by the need for a one-time manual MuJoCo calibration in the vision-based hardware experiments.}
